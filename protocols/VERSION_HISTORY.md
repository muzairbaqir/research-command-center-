## Versioning Rules

1. **Major Increments (`v1.0`, `v2.0`)**: Fundamental structural changes, new market scope, or comprehensive redesign of opportunity/label logic.
2. **Minor Increments (`v1.1`, `v1.2`)**: Additions of features, refined cost parameters, or adjusted filters approved via formal change requests.
3. **Draft Stems (`v0.x`)**: Unfrozen development versions prior to initial research freeze.
